About Me

Tutoring & Coaching Approach

My goal is to help students understand what they're learning, develop the skills to work through challenging problems, and become more confident and independent learners.

  1. Breaking Down Difficult Problems helps students approach challenging problems by breaking them into manageable steps.

  2. Focusing on Understanding emphasizes comprehension of instruction rather than memorization.

  3. Personalized Instruction means lessons are based on the student's goals and individual needs.

  4. Building Independence helps students become more confident, capable, and independent learners.
