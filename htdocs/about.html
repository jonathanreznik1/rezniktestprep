About Me

Hi, I'm Yoni Reznik.

When I started my tutoring and test prep business, I discovered a calling for empowering K–12 students of all ages and levels to achieve their best and gain confidence by engaging meaningfully with math and computer science.

My Background

My own interest in STEM led me to many fascinating experiences, first academically at MIT, where I earned a B.S. in Economics in 2004, and then professionally in fields spanning finance, scientific research, and software systems. I also held an internship with NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory in its Science division. More recently, I have worked in data science and analytics, as well as in the classroom as an educator at several local schools, including Highland Hall and YULA Girls.

My Approach to Teaching & Tutoring

As a tutor with 15 years of experience, I have worked with students across a wide range of ages, abilities, and academic needs. I pride myself on positively supporting and encouraging students, while helping them take practical steps that can improve their performance in class and build confidence in how they approach a subject.

My own passion for STEM helps me relate to students and find ways to encourage their own interest in learning and succeeding. To get the most out of our time together, I meet students where they are and am willing to look beyond formal methods when a different way of thinking can help them understand the material.

Math lends itself particularly well to this, as does standardized test preparation, because there is often more than one way to approach a problem or answer a question. While students must learn to respect their teachers' methods of instruction, I have found that time outside the classroom can provide an opportunity to explore alternative approaches and then connect those approaches back to what students are learning in class.

Building Confidence Through Learning

Over the years, I have built lasting relationships with students and families who have trusted me to help them plan, prepare, and develop the skills they need to perform at their best on both subject-matter and standardized tests.
